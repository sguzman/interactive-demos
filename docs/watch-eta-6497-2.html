DOCUMENTATION · PROVENANCE · M6E

ETA / Unitas 6497-2

M6e closes the current normalized movement architecture and resolves the train power path into named reconstructed stages. Barrel torque, stage-resolved train load, dynamic feedback, finite escapement work, integrated balance state, release timing, reserve and explicit stall modes now participate in one causal loop.

Accuracy contract.

The current official reference remains 3 Hz / 21,600 A/h, 17 jewels, 44° lift angle, and 53 h minimum / 60 h typical reserve. ETA's dated 2020 manufacturing sheet also gives 25 winding-stem turns for complete winding; the public winding-input model now uses that P0 dated anchor. M6 barrel-drum release turns, torque, load, stage efficiency, work and stall values remain educational reconstruction parameters—not ETA production measurements.

Closed-loop causal chain

crown → arbor winding → spring twist → barrel torque → stage-resolved train load → compounded transmission → available escapement work → finite polygon work transfer → delivered Δω → integrated balance θ/ω → pallet / escape release → train → hands

Downstream demand also feeds back upstream: rejected work, low amplitude, geometry blockage and stall state raise reaction load, reducing torque margin and available impulse.

M6a — arbor / drum power separation

M6a distinguishes crown/ratchet-driven barrel-arbor winding from slow barrel-drum release. The winding-input endpoint is now calibrated to ETA's dated 2020 statement of 25 winding-stem turns to complete winding. That does not determine barrel-drum rundown turns or mainspring development turns. Reserve becomes normalized spring twist; that twist feeds a reconstructed torque curve; load and transmission reduce that torque before it reaches the escapement work layer.

M6b — dynamic reaction load

M6b lets downstream mechanical demand push back toward the barrel. Running demand, delivered escapement work, rejected-work backpressure, low oscillator amplitude, unhealthy polygon geometry and stall state contribute to a smoothed reaction load.

drive margin = spring torque − dynamic train load

post-load drive = positive drive margin × load-dependent transmission

Reserve and usable power are therefore separate: the movement can stop with reserve remaining if the reconstructed load consumes the available torque margin.

M6c — shared work ledger

spring-side budget − train/load loss − contact loss − balance-delivered work = ledger residual

The residual is shown explicitly and should numerically close around zero. These remain normalized opportunity units, not joules.

M6c also exposes an 8-turn normalized spring-differential presentation state. It is not a measured 6497-2 mainspring turn count.

M6d — movement-level coordinator

M6d reads the barrel, load-feedback, ledger, polygon-contact, oscillator and power-gate states as one movement. It distinguishes states such as RUNNING, WINDING, PAUSED, TORQUE STALL, GEOMETRY BLOCKED, OSCILLATOR STALLED and UNWOUND.

Torque stall is hysteretic: it enters below roughly 0.018 normalized drive margin and remains latched until winding or load relief restores about 0.055. This prevents simulation chatter around a single threshold.

M6e — stage-resolved train path

M6e replaces another anonymous scalar with named mechanical stages matching the active reference train topology.

The stage efficiencies multiply into a transmission ceiling. M6b then applies additional load-dependent degradation below that ceiling. Dynamic running, delivered-work demand, rejected-work backpressure, low amplitude, geometry blockage and stall penalties are added mainly on the escapement/load side.

The important architectural benefit is replaceability: future measured or better-founded losses can be attached to specific stages instead of replacing one opaque train-efficiency number.

Physical amplitude vs normalized oscillator state

The public oscillator uses integrated angle/velocity state and a normalized 0–1 amplitude coordinate. Its 0.43 rad visual/state scale is a reconstruction parameter, not the physical maximum swing of the real balance. ETA's dated 2020 adjustment sheet instead gives real timegrapher-style amplitude criteria reaching 320° horizontally and minima of 210° / 200° vertically under specified wind-state conditions.

Therefore normalized amplitude, visual balance angle, and physical balance amplitude in degrees are three different quantities.

Materials, surfaces and service state

Exact ETA 6497-2 documentation identifies brass plates/bridges, a steel escape wheel and pallet fork, a gilt Glucydur balance, and a nickel-steel balance spring. Current service documentation also distinguishes thin oil, high-pressure oil, greases, pallet-stone escapement lubricant and Fixodrop epilame treatment.

The complete movement barrel is treated by ETA as a service-preserved prelubricated assembly: the current communication marks it as a do-not-wash part and directs replacement with an original prelubricated ETA part when required.

Service state is mechanical state.

Cleanliness, epilame condition, lubricant identity/amount, contamination, wear and assembly condition can change behavior even when component identity is unchanged. Lubricant names are not converted into invented friction coefficients.

M5 escapement causality retained

M6 supplies upstream mechanics; it does not replace the M5 escapement. The live movement still uses integrated θ/ω balance state, finite escape-tooth and pallet-jewel polygons, geometry-derived lock/impulse/drop/capture, polygon surface-follow distance, variable work-scaled Δω, and geometry-derived escape release as the downstream train's timing source.

What is now genuinely coupled

At the normalized educational level the simulation connects winding input, spring state, barrel torque, arbor/drum roles, stage-resolved reaction load, downstream load feedback, transmission, finite escapement contact, available/delivered/lost work, oscillator angular state, torque/geometry/amplitude stall states, escape release, wheel-train progress and displayed time.

What remains approximate

  • barrel torque is dimensionless rather than measured N·mm;
  • mainspring active-turn geometry and torque-deflection data are not measured;
  • stage loads and efficiencies are reconstruction parameters, not measured train-loss data;
  • per-mesh wheel/pinion inertia and tooth-force vectors are not solved;
  • pivot, jewel, gear and lubrication friction are not calibrated;
  • pallet contact work remains normalized rather than joules;
  • elastic impact, contact stress and compliance are not solved;
  • balance inertia and hairspring stiffness / terminal geometry are not calibrated;
  • positional, regulator and temperature effects are not yet part of the dynamic system;
  • production amplitude and timing performance are not claimed.

These are now primarily calibration/fidelity problems attached to an established causal architecture rather than missing connections among independent animations.

Inspection workflow

Wind the assembled movement in Winding view, then switch to Escapement and run at 0.1× or 0.25×. Compare the M6e stage load with M6b drive margin, M5i available/delivered/lost work, M6c ledger closure and M5f oscillator Δω/amplitude. M6d reports the movement-level operating or fault state.

Future deepening after M6

The next major step is physical validation against an identified 6497-2 movement. The validation plan targets winding count, rundown reserve, timegrapher rate/amplitude/beat error, train tooth counts, dimensional geometry, service state, barrel/mainspring geometry and model-vs-specimen residuals. Until such a specimen is measured, unresolved physical values remain unknown rather than being back-filled from the visualization.
