ETA / Unitas 6497-2

A movement-first interactive reconstruction of a large hand-wound mechanical wristwatch, using the ETA/Unitas 6497-2 as the mechanical target and the Panerai OP XI / PAM111 lineage as a well-documented wristwatch reference frame.

Accuracy contract.

The demo is not manufacturing CAD. Every important component is meant to declare whether it is sourced from official data, reconstructed from reference material, approximate, or purely presentational. Increased realism must not erase that distinction.

Why this target

The 6497 family is unusually good for an interactive explainer: it is large, manually wound, mechanically legible, widely serviced, and extensively photographed. The OP XI lineage also places that architecture inside a large exhibition-back wristwatch, which gives the demo a concrete product context without requiring us to invent the entire watch from scratch.

Official movement facts

Diameter36.60 mm
Movement height4.50 mm
Frequency3 Hz
Alternations21,600 A/h
Jewels17
Power reserve53 h min / 60 h typical

ETA's 2025 technical communication additionally gives a typical balance lift angle of 44°. The product portal identifies the movement as manual-wind, Lépine architecture, with hours, minutes and small seconds.

Reference watch

The shell currently targets a 44 mm exhibition-style Luminor Marina / PAM111 reference lineage. The PAM111's OP XI movement is documented as a decorated and modified Unitas 6497-2 derivative. The first reconstruction prioritizes movement architecture over exact case revision, dial printing, engravings, or proprietary crown-guard CAD.

Functional assemblies

  1. Case / protection — cushion case, bezel, crystal, crown guard and exhibition back.
  2. Display — dial, central hour/minute hands and small seconds at 9 o'clock.
  3. Motion works — cannon pinion and hour-wheel reduction.
  4. Winding — stem, crown wheel, ratchet wheel and click.
  5. Power — barrel, arbor and mainspring.
  6. Wheel train — centre, third, fourth/seconds and escape wheels.
  7. Escapement — escape wheel, pallet fork and stones.
  8. Oscillator / regulation — balance, hairspring and regulator.
  9. Structure — mainplate, bridges, cock, jewels and screws.

Constructive geometry policy

Authored dimensions are expressed in millimetres and generated through reusable primitives rather than hidden baked meshes wherever practical. The current vocabulary includes disc, ring, roundedPlate, caseRing, polygonPlate, gear, pinion, screw, jewel, coil, pathTube, and parameterized hands.

This makes the model inspectable and editable. Boolean CSG can be added later if it substantially improves bridge, plate, or case fidelity.

Provenance classes

official Dimension or fact directly supported by ETA/Panerai technical material.

reference-derived Reconstructed from service diagrams, teardown photographs, or multiple references.

approximate Simplified geometry intended to preserve function and relative placement.

presentation Geometry added for readability, lighting, or visual communication.

Current milestone: M1 architecture

M1 establishes the real movement dimensions, functional system boundaries, a recognizable 6497-style layout, hard movable inspection lighting, explicit provenance, and an exploded-view grammar. The gear train still uses visualization-speed animation rather than a solved mechanical state model, and bridge contours remain reference-derived approximations.

Next realism milestones

M2 — bridge and plate fidelity

Replace generic bridge silhouettes with closer reference-derived contours, improve screw/jewel placement, and model more of the visible machining relief.

M3 — train fidelity

Refine wheel diameters, pinions, arbor relationships, gear pairing, and the small-seconds output using technical drawings and teardown references.

M4 — winding fidelity

Make crown rotation causal: crown/stem drives winding works, ratchet turns the barrel arbor, click blocks reversal, and a normalized mainspring-energy state increases.

M5 — escapement fidelity

Improve escape tooth geometry, pallet locking and impulse faces, banking, balance staff, hairspring presentation, and visualized impulse transfer.

M6 — system simulation

Derive the wheel train and hands from a shared mechanical state instead of independent decorative animation.

Lighting

The demo is designed as an inspection tool rather than only a beauty render. The default key is intentionally hard. Its azimuth, elevation, distance and intensity can be changed, while presets provide raking side light, top inspection, backlight, studio and dark presentation modes.
