DOCUMENTATION · PROVENANCE · M5A

ETA / Unitas 6497-2

M5a makes the Swiss lever escapement an explicit event sequence. The balance cadence now drives lock, unlock, impulse and relock; escape-wheel release in turn meters the visible progress of the rest of the train.

Accuracy contract.

This remains an educational reconstruction, not manufacturing CAD or a rigid-body contact simulation. Official movement facts, reference-derived geometry and presentation assumptions stay explicitly separated.

Foundation retained

The M3 train reconstruction and M4 winding/setting/power systems remain intact: reference-derived tooth-count topology, pitch/contact diagnostics, separate wheel planes, staffs and jewel spans, endshake diagnostics, causal crown winding, two stem positions, persistent mainspring reserve, and an energy gate that stops an unwound movement.

M5a — six beat events per second

A 3 Hz balance completes three full oscillations per second, producing six alternations / beats per second. M5a treats one beat as 1/6 s of mechanical time. Balance center crossings define the cadence for the escapement state machine.

Lock → unlock → impulse → relock

Each beat now passes through four visible states:

  1. LOCK · ENTRY — the escape wheel is held;
  2. UNLOCK — the pallet leaves the locked tooth;
  3. IMPULSE — the escape wheel advances while the fork crosses toward the opposite bank;
  4. LOCK · EXIT — the opposite pallet holds the next tooth until the next beat.

The exact fractional timing of these windows is reconstruction timing chosen to make the mechanism inspectable. It is not presented as ETA production lock/lift geometry.

Half-tooth escape release

Escape wheel15 teeth
Release per beat½ tooth
Angular release12° / beat

Thirty half-tooth releases make one full escape-wheel revolution. At six beats per second, that reproduces the existing 5 s/rev reference period. The wheel is now visibly stationary in the lock windows and advances only through the reconstructed release window.

Architectural change.

The train no longer merely shares a clock with the escapement. M5a converts released escape-wheel angle back into released train time. While the escape wheel is locked, the fourth/seconds, third and centre wheels and the hands wait. During release, they advance. The escapement is therefore now the timing gate for visible train progress.

Added escapement geometry

The M5a overlay adds a roller table, guard roller, ruby impulse jewel, fork horns, safety dart, banking pins, optional escape → pallet → balance center-line guides, and a brief impulse-contact marker. These parts are reference-derived / presentation geometry: their functional roles are real, while exact dimensions and face clearances remain future work.

Inspection controls

The mechanical time selector now includes 0.1×, 0.25× and 0.5× slow-motion modes. At 0× paused, the Step exactly one beat control advances the energy-gated mechanism by one 1/6 s beat, making the release sequence easy to inspect. The Escapement camera preset automatically enables the center-line guide overlay.

What M5a still simplifies

  • exact entry/exit locking-face and impulse-face angles;
  • draw, drop and lock depth;
  • exact banking-pin placement;
  • exact roller-jewel / fork-slot geometry and horn safety clearances;
  • contact friction and lubrication;
  • impulse energy transfer into balance amplitude;
  • free balance amplitude determined by spring torque and losses;
  • production torque transfer through the train.

The oscillator frequency is still prescribed from the official 3 Hz rate. M5a makes the release sequence event-driven and causal at the topology/timing level; it does not yet solve contact mechanics.

Earlier causal milestones retained

M4a — winding

Crown input advances the crown wheel and ratchet, the click enforces one-way behavior, and accepted winding accumulates stored reserve. The current full-wind mapping uses 45 crown turns as a presentation assumption; the 60 h typical reserve endpoint is sourced from ETA.

M4b — setting

Position 0 winds. Position 1 pulls the reconstructed stem outward, shifts the keyless selector geometry, disables winding and routes input into hand setting.

M4c — power gate

The watch starts stopped and unwound. Positive reserve permits mechanical time to advance; running consumes reserve; zero reserve freezes it.

Next work

M5b — pallet-face and safety geometry

Explicit entry/exit locking and impulse faces, improved roller-jewel/fork-slot geometry, more defensible banking limits, and diagnostic lock-depth/drop visualization.

M5c — impulse / oscillator coupling

Deliver normalized impulse packets at each beat, allow delivered impulse to change balance amplitude, and let low reserve eventually make unlocking unreliable instead of prescribing perfect amplitude forever.

M6 — system simulation

Separate barrel-arbor winding from barrel-drum release, improve power transmission through the train, and couple reserve, amplitude and rate into one system state.
