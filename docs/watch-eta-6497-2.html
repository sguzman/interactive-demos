DOCUMENTATION · PROVENANCE · M4C

ETA / Unitas 6497-2

M4c closes the first causal power loop. Winding creates reserve; reserve permits one shared mechanical clock to advance; running consumes reserve; zero reserve stops the balance, escapement presentation, wheel train and hands.

Geometry foundation

The M3 train reconstruction remains intact: centre 80 teeth, third 60 / 10-leaf pinion, seconds/fourth 120 / 8-leaf pinion, escape 15 / 10-leaf pinion, with pitch/contact diagnostics, separate wheel planes, stepped staffs, jewel spans, endshake guides and wheel-body gap diagnostics. M4a — causal winding

Positive crown input advances the crown wheel and ratchet, the click enforces one-way behavior, and accepted winding increases a persistent normalized mainspring-energy state. The blue barrel overlay visualizes that state. The interaction maps full wind to 45 crown turns; that number is a presentation assumption, while the 60 h typical reserve is sourced from ETA.

M4b — two stem positions

Position 0 retains winding. Position 1 pulls the reconstructed crown/stem outward, shifts the sliding-pinion/yoke/setting-lever presentation, disables winding and routes input into the visible setting path. The current educational chain uses 14 teeth for the sliding pinion, 22 for the setting wheel and 28 for the minute wheel, with a 1.35 mm presentation pull distance.

M4c — energy-gated movement

The watch now starts stopped and unwound. It no longer advances simply because browser time is passing. Instead:

  1. winding creates stored reserve;
  2. positive reserve opens the power gate;
  3. one shared mechanical elapsed-time state advances;
  4. balance, pallet presentation, escape wheel, fourth/seconds wheel, third wheel, centre wheel and all three hands derive from that mechanical clock;
  5. running consumes reserve;
  6. zero reserve freezes the mechanical clock and stops the movement.
The old contradiction is gone.

An unwound watch no longer keeps running. Give it a little wind and it starts; let reserve reach zero and it stops at the exact mechanical state it had reached.

Reserve depletion model

M4c uses a deliberately simple normalized model. Stored energy maps linearly to the official 60 h typical reserve endpoint. At 1×, one simulated second consumes one second of that nominal reserve. Winding while running can replenish reserve. This is not a torque curve: declining mainspring torque, friction, amplitude decay and escapement efficiency are not yet calculated.

Mechanical time scale

The former train-only speed control now scales the shared mechanical clock, so visible motion and reserve depletion remain causally consistent.

0×paused
1×real time
10–300×inspection
3600×1 sim h / real s

At 3600×, a full nominal 60 h reserve can be observed running down in about one real minute. That is explicitly simulation-time acceleration, not watch behavior.

What is still deliberately simplified

  • mainspring torque curve and barrel-arbor/drum force transfer;
  • friction through the train;
  • escapement efficiency and discrete impulse physics;
  • balance amplitude decay and positional rate error;
  • exact clutch/Breguet-tooth geometry;
  • exact crown-turn count to full wind.

Inspection tools retained

Train mesh, Train stack, Keyless works, pitch/contact guides, staff/clearance guides, endshake diagnostics, wheel-body gap guides, camera-aligned inspection lighting and the movable manual key all remain available.
