DOCUMENTATION · PROVENANCE · M4A

ETA / Unitas 6497-2

M4a is the first user-operated causal subsystem: crown input now drives the crown wheel and ratchet, the click blocks reverse ratchet motion, and accepted winding accumulates a visible stored mainspring-energy state.

Accuracy contract.

The demo is not manufacturing CAD. Official movement facts remain separate from reference-derived reconstruction geometry and presentation assumptions. The 45-crown-turn full-wind mapping used for interaction is explicitly not an ETA service specification.

Geometry foundation retained from M3

The current reconstruction keeps the reference-derived 6497 train topology: centre 80 teeth, third 60 / 10-leaf pinion, seconds/fourth 120 / 8-leaf pinion, escape 15 / 10-leaf pinion. Pitch-radius-native train geometry, separate compound-wheel planes, stepped staffs, jewel spans, pitch/contact guides, endshake diagnostics and wheel-body gap diagnostics all remain available.

M4a — interactive crown

Wind crown advances the crown in the winding direction; Return crown rotates the crown side back. Both controls can be clicked for a visible step or held continuously. Keyboard shortcuts are W and R.

An indexed crown overlay makes axial rotation legible even though the crown itself is round.

Crown wheel → ratchet

Visible crown wheel34 teeth
Visible ratchet44 teeth
Directionone-way storage

The interaction model uses that visible 34:44 relationship to advance the ratchet during positive winding. It is a causal reconstruction relation, not a complete manufacturing model of every intermediate keyless-work detail.

One-way click constraint

The old decorative ratchet oscillation is gone. Ratchet angle now persists. Forward input can advance it; return input can reverse the crown/crown-wheel side but cannot back-drive the ratchet. The UI reports the click as RATCHETING, LOCKED, or SEATED.

The reconstructed click receives a deliberately tiny tooth-lift motion during forward winding and remains seated on return.

Stored mainspring energy

Accepted winding increments a normalized energy state from 0 to 1. The demo exposes that state as stored-energy percentage, equivalent reserve scaled to the official 60 h typical figure, accumulated ratchet-click count, and a blue procedural mainspring overlay that contracts and intensifies as energy rises.

Interaction assumption.

The current model maps full wind to 45 crown turns so the mechanism can be explored comfortably. That number is a presentation/reconstruction assumption. The 60 h typical reserve endpoint is the sourced ETA figure.

Full-wind stop

When the normalized energy state reaches 1.0, further positive winding is blocked. Return motion remains available. This gives the winding subsystem a meaningful terminal state instead of permitting infinite decorative rotation.

What is still deliberately uncoupled

M4a does not yet claim a production torque curve, friction model, exact crown-turn count, barrel bridle behavior, or escapement losses. Most importantly, stored energy does not yet gate train release: the existing train and hand timing graph can continue to run at zero stored energy. That coupling is reserved for the next power-release stage rather than being silently faked.

Inspection tools retained

  • Train mesh — pitch circles, centre lines and tangent/contact markers.
  • Train stack — side-on staffs, jewel regions and bridge/mainplate clearances.
  • Endshake guides with 1× nominal and exaggerated diagnostic motion.
  • Wheel-body axial-gap guides.

Lighting

Camera-aligned balanced lighting remains the default, with a movable manual key, combined mode, large intensity/exposure range, and the intentionally excessive Full bright / diagnostic preset.
