M3f — pitch-radius-native train teeth

The older constructive gear primitive did not interpret its radius as a strict pitch radius. M3f adds a train-only spur construction where the supplied radius is the pitch radius. Addendum and dedendum now bracket that pitch line, and tooth width is tied to circular pitch.

Still educational, not involute CAD.

The new tooth flanks are simplified tapered/polygonal approximations. The important improvement is semantic: solved pitch circles now correspond directly to rendered tooth engagement instead of being translated through an ambiguous decorative radius.

Pitch/contact guides

Show pitch mesh guides displays both pitch circles, their line of centres, and a bright tangent/contact marker for each wheel→pinion mesh. The Train mesh camera preset enables this automatically.

M3f — stepped staffs and pivots

The old uniform rods are hidden. The centre, third, seconds/fourth and escape assemblies now sit on schematic stepped staffs with a mainplate pivot, central arbor, wheel-seat collar, bridge neck and small polished upper pivot reaching the visible bridge jewel region.

Train stack rotates to a side-on inspection view and automatically enables Show staff / clearance guides. Those guides expose the reconstructed mainplate plane, bridge underside and vertical staff lines so the axial architecture can be inspected instead of inferred.

Lighting

The balanced camera-axis setup remains unchanged from the previous correction: a moderate default, movable manual key, and intentionally excessive Full bright / diagnostic headroom when required.
