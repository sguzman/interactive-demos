ETA / Unitas 6497-2

A movement-first interactive reconstruction of a large hand-wound mechanical watch. M3d gives the compound train explicit axial planes while the new camera-axis lighting makes the model usable as an inspection object.

Accuracy contract.

The demo is not manufacturing CAD. Important facts and geometry remain classified as official, reference-derived, approximate, or presentational.

M3a — reference timing

The train uses a published 6497 training-tool ratio set: centre 80 teeth, third 60 teeth / 10-leaf pinion, seconds/fourth 120 teeth / 8-leaf pinion, and escape 15 teeth / 10-leaf pinion. At 21,600 A/h this closes to 5 s/rev for the escape wheel, 60 s/rev for the seconds/fourth wheel, 7.5 min/rev for the third wheel, and 1 h/rev for the centre wheel.

M3b — visible tooth and leaf counts

The rendered train uses those same reference counts rather than placeholder counts.

M3c — pitch-derived geometry

For each external mesh the reconstruction derives a nominal module from the current centre distance and tooth/leaf counts using module = 2 × centre_distance / (wheel_teeth + pinion_leaves).

M3d — compound train planes

Large pitch-derived wheel bodies cannot all occupy the same z-plane. M3d now assigns explicit bridge-side wheel planes:

Centre wheelz = -0.65 mm
Third wheelz = -1.10 mm
Seconds/fourthz = -1.55 mm
Escape wheelz = -0.65 mm

The third pinion lands in the centre-wheel plane, the seconds/fourth pinion in the third-wheel plane, and the escape pinion in the seconds/fourth-wheel plane. Those heights are still reconstruction values, but the axial relationships are now mechanically purposeful rather than decorative.

Camera-axis inspection lighting

Camera aligned mode conceptually places a directional light just behind the camera and aims it through the orbit target. That gives predictable frontal illumination without inverse-square brightness loss as the camera moves.

  • Camera aligned, Manual key only, and Camera + manual key modes.
  • Camera and manual intensities each adjustable up to 700.
  • Ambient/fill up to 1.20 and exposure up to 3.20×.
  • Full bright / diagnostic preset for geometry-first inspection.
  • Neutral reflection environment, stronger broad fill, rim light and lighter backboard.

Simulation presentation

The train can run at 1× real time or 10×, 60× and 300× inspection scales. The balance remains at the documented 3 Hz.
