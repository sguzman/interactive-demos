DOCUMENTATION · PROVENANCE · M4B

ETA / Unitas 6497-2

M4b turns the crown into a two-state control: pushed in for winding, pulled out for hand setting. The reconstructed sliding pinion, yoke and setting lever now visibly transition between those modes.

Accuracy contract.

The demo is not manufacturing CAD. Official movement facts remain separate from reference-derived reconstruction geometry and presentation assumptions. Stem travel and setting ratios in M4b are deliberately labeled as reconstruction values.

M4a winding remains active

In stem Position 0 the demo retains the causal M4a winding state: crown input advances crown wheel and ratchet, the click blocks reverse ratchet motion, and accepted winding accumulates a normalized mainspring-energy state. The 45-turn full-wind mapping remains an interaction assumption rather than an ETA specification.

M4b — two stem positions

Position 0Winding
Position 1Hand setting
Visible pull1.35 mm reconstruction

Pulling to Position 1 translates the visible crown/stem outward, disables the winding controls, shifts the reconstructed sliding pinion toward the setting wheel, and moves the yoke and setting-lever geometry with the selection state.

Setting path

Once the stem is in Position 1, the setting controls rotate the visible keyless wheels and change the hour/minute display without adding mainspring energy. A dedicated Keyless works inspection camera centers the dial-side mechanism.

Sliding pinion14 teeth
Setting wheel22 teeth
Minute wheel28 teeth

Those constructive counts drive the visible intermediate-wheel rotations. M4b then uses minute-wheel rotation as a simplified proxy for the final cannon-pinion/minute-hand setting displacement. That last mapping is a reconstruction simplification, not a production ratio claim.

Mode isolation

  • Winding position can add mainspring energy and cannot use the setting controls.
  • Setting position can move the hour/minute hands and cannot add mainspring energy.
  • Returning to winding position preserves the time offset set in Position 1.

Geometry foundation retained

The M3 train reconstruction remains: true-pitch wheel/pinion semantics, constrained compound-wheel planes, stepped staffs, jewel spans, pitch/contact diagnostics, endshake inspection and wheel-body gap guides.

What is still deliberately approximate

M4b does not yet claim exact ETA stem travel, clutch/Breguet tooth geometry, yoke/setting-lever pivots, keyless friction/backlash, or the exact production setting ratio. The barrel still does not gate train motion; that is M4c.

Lighting

Camera-aligned balanced lighting remains the default, with a movable manual key, combined mode, large intensity/exposure range, and the intentionally excessive Full bright / diagnostic preset.
