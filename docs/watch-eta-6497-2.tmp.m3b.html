ETA / Unitas 6497-2

A movement-first interactive reconstruction of a large hand-wound mechanical watch. M3b aligns the visible train with the same reference tooth/leaf counts used by the timing graph and rebuilds the lighting so the model works as an inspection tool rather than a dark beauty render.

Accuracy contract.

The demo is not manufacturing CAD. Every important fact or geometry claim should remain distinguishable as official, reference-derived, approximate, or presentational.

  • Done: separate barrel, train, pallet and balance bridges.
  • Done: documented bridge screw counts represented.
  • Done: train-bridge jewel positions aligned to the visible train pivots.

M3a — reference train kinematics

The train no longer uses arbitrary independent animation speeds. kinematics.js derives periods from a published 6497 training-tool ratio set and validates the closure at startup.

Escape15 teeth · 5 s/rev
Seconds / fourth120 teeth · 60 s/rev
Third60 teeth · 7.5 min/rev
Centre80 teeth · 1 h/rev
Fourth pinion8 leaves
Third / escape pinions10 leaves

The same pass corrected the escapement timing model: a Swiss lever releases the escape wheel by half a tooth per beat. At six beats per second with 15 escape teeth, the escape wheel completes one revolution in five seconds.

M3b — visible train geometry

The visible centre, third, seconds/fourth and escape wheels now declare the same reference counts as the timing graph. The third wheel carries a 10-leaf visual pinion, the seconds/fourth wheel an 8-leaf pinion, and the escape wheel a 10-leaf pinion. Legacy placeholder pinion meshes are hidden when the M3b geometry is applied.

Still not CAD.

The radii and centre positions are not yet solved from a defensible module/centre-distance model. M3b aligns count and topology first. A later train-layout pass will solve pitch radii, staff heights and meshing geometry rather than quietly implying exact placement.

Lighting rebuild

The earlier rig was genuinely too dark. There were two technical causes: the metal-heavy PBR materials had no environment map to reflect, and the SpotLight UI value was being used directly even though inverse-square falloff made a value around 145 extremely weak at the model's working distance.

The rebuilt inspection rig now provides:

  • a neutral studio reflection environment for steel, brass and gilt materials;
  • a substantially scaled movable spot key with real distance falloff;
  • a broad directional fill so non-highlighted metal faces do not collapse to black;
  • a camera-following inspection light for front/rear inspection;
  • exposure control in addition to key intensity and ambient/fill;
  • a Full bright / diagnostic preset intended to make geometry readable even when beauty-light presets are unhelpful.

Inspection and simulation controls

Camera presets cover overview, bridge side, dial side, escapement and winding. The train can remain at 1× real time or be accelerated to 10×, 60× or 300× for visual inspection. This speed multiplier is explicitly presentational; the balance remains at the documented 3 Hz.

Constructive geometry

Authored geometry is expressed in millimetres and generated from reusable primitives including disc, ring, roundedPlate, caseRing, polygonPlate, plateWithHoles, gear, escapeWheel, pinion, screw, jewel, shockSetting, coil, pathTube and parameterized hands.

Provenance classes

approximate Simplified geometry preserving role and relative placement.

Next work

M3c — train layout fidelity

Derive more defensible pitch radii and centre distances, improve staff heights and wheel layering, and make the visual mesh relationships correspond to the ratio graph rather than merely sharing counts.

M4 — winding fidelity

Make crown/stem position stateful, distinguish winding from hand-setting, transmit crown rotation through the keyless works and ratchet, animate the click as a one-way constraint, and store mainspring energy.

M5 — escapement fidelity

Model locking and impulse faces, banking and roller-jewel interaction so escape-wheel motion becomes causal rather than beat-indexed.

M6 — system simulation

Unify barrel energy, train ratios, escapement release, balance state and hand positions into one causal model.
