Power note: the operational shaft receives a deliberately slowed P5 continuous-motion presentation while powered. Power-off blocks ordinary character and powered operational actions rather than merely freezing the display.

Cover note: the hinged service-cover motion is a P5 presentation path kept separate from exploded inspection and from the live mechanical state. Opening it exposes the carrier/type-element region without moving the frame.

Paper-path note: paper release moves the front and rear feed-roll banks together. Platen variable is a distinct coupling state: when free, manual platen rotation no longer moves the ratchet. Release travel and hidden paper-feed centers remain P4/P5 rather than factory dimensions.

Horizontal-motion note: tab stops are shown on the 12-CPI writing lattice with a P5 default every eight positions. Tab travel is mainspring-propelled in the real mechanism; the public action now sweeps to the next default stop while the cord/drum geometry updates from the same carrier coordinate. Carrier return terminates at the left margin and performs one 27-tooth-profile index step.

Selection note: keyboard characters use a deterministic P5 mapping into the 4 × 22 type-element slot structure so the mechanism can be exercised before one exact physical typeball layout is frozen. The mechanical selection ranges themselves are canonical: tilt bands 0–3, rotate units −5…+5, plus the independent 180° shift hemisphere.

Exact factory geometry is not claimed for unresolved hidden sections. P4 parts preserve the active branch, known dimensions, service topology and mechanical relationships while the reconstruction is refined.
