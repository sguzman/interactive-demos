BALANCED DEFAULT
M5a replaces the old pallet/escape-wheel choreography with an event-resolved educational Swiss-lever state machine. The balance crossing selects alternating pallets; lock, unlock and impulse windows govern the escape wheel; released escape motion is converted back into train time so the fourth, third, centre wheels and hands visibly wait on escapement release. Exact pallet-face angles, draw, drop and impulse geometry are still reconstruction-level and remain future M5 work.
